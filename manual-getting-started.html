Getting Started

The general menu is the starting point for every area of Smart-Score.

Open the side menu

Tap the hamburger menu icon in the upper-left corner of the app. The side menu slides into view and gives you access to new matches, pool disciplines, the archive, monitoring, settings, and app information.

New Match

Tap one of the disciplines —14.1, 8-Ball, 9-Ball, or 10-Ball—to start a new match of that discipline. Smart-Score then opens the corresponding page and starts the match.

Administration

  • Match Archive opens an overview of past matches and their results.
  • Monitoring shows currently active matches from other tables.
  • Settings opens the application configuration.
  • Info displays application and version information.
